## UI Tone Guidelines

Even as an internal tool, the copy inside the app should feel consistent with the Raphael brand concept — an intelligent, calm, decisive advisor.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Empty states | Helpful, action-oriented | "No tasks yet. Add your first task to get started." |
| Error messages | Calm, direct, specific | "Couldn't save the task. Check your connection and try again." |
| AI advisor briefings | Confident, clear, concise | "3 tasks are overdue on the electrical project. Address those first." |
| Success confirmations | Brief, forward-looking | "Task moved to Done." |
| Onboarding | Encouraging, minimal | "Welcome. Your projects are empty — create your first one." |

**Voice rules**:
- Use "you" and "your", not "user" or "the user"
- Lead with the action or outcome, not the feature name
- Keep messages short — one sentence where possible
- Never use filler words: "successfully", "please", "kindly"
